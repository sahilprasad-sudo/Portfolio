Privacy Policy for Traveller

Effective Date: November 13, 2026 • Developer: Sahil Prasad

Traveller ("we", "our", or "the app") is an iOS expedition planner and group expense tracker. This Privacy Policy explains what information Traveller collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Information: When you create an account or sign in using Email/Password, Sign in with Apple, or Google Sign-In, we collect your name, username, email address, optional bio, and unique user identifier (UID).
  • Trip & Expense Content: Trips, itinerary stops, checkpoint notes, member names, and group expense records you create in the app are stored locally on your device and, when cloud sync is enabled, synced to your Firebase account so your data stays available across devices.
  • Authentication & Diagnostics: Basic authentication tokens and standard Firebase operational data needed to keep you signed in securely and sync your trips reliably.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and display your profile inside the app.
  • To store, sync, and restore your trip itineraries and group expense settlements.
  • To respond to support requests you send to us.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal information, show third-party ads, or track you across other companies' apps or websites. Traveller relies on the following trusted infrastructure providers solely to operate core app features:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): For secure login and trip/profile cloud sync.
  • Apple & Google Sign-In: When you choose to authenticate with your Apple ID or Google account.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Your account and synced data are retained only as long as your account remains active. You can permanently delete your account and associated profile data at any time directly inside the app by going to Settings → Delete Account. If you have trouble accessing the app, you may also email us to request deletion.

5. Children's Privacy

Traveller is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact Sahil Prasad at privatesahilprasad@gmail.com or visit https://sahilprasad.in/traveller/support.
